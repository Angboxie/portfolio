01 / Overview

Place. Battle. Win.

FigmaGame UIPixel artPrototypingUX flowCharacter designAI art direction

Tactibrawl is a pixel-art tactical brawler designed in Figma. Two players place heroes on a grid and battle for control of the arena.

I led the UI across a 5-person team: splash, loading, hero select, tutorial and battle arena. Characters were hand-drawn in pixel art. Environments were AI-generated, then curated and art-directed.

The whole game is prototyped and playable in Figma.

06 / Process

Six steps.

01
  1. 01

    Game concept + UX flow

    Mapped the full loop as a team: splash, hero select, tutorial, grid placement, battle. Every screen defined before design started.

  2. 02

    Art direction

    Pixel characters on painterly AI-generated environments. Gold as the single UI accent. Fantasy, without the generic RPG look.

  3. 03

    Character design

    Tank, Healer, Mage and Sola, drawn pixel by pixel. Silhouettes that stay readable at small grid sizes in battle.

  4. 04

    Environments

    AI-generated backgrounds, heavily curated: forest arena, waterfall canyon, training room. Each refined to sit with the pixel art without clashing.

  5. 05

    Tutorial system

    A dialogue-driven tutorial with Sola as the guide. Her expressions react to context. Parchment dialogue boxes match the game world.

  6. 06

    Full prototype

    Every screen wired with interactions and transitions. Playable from title screen to battle in Figma.
