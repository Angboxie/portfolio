Casepop AU.

01 / Overview

Art in your pocket.

ShopifyLiquidHTML / CSS / JSBrand designUGC strategyE-commerce

The phone case market is flooded with mass production. Local Australian artists were making great work with no platform built to sell it at scale. So we built one.

Every design comes from an Australian artist. We run the store and the growth; the artist gets the credit.

I owned all front-end work: custom code on top of Shopify, built for what the brand needed rather than what a template offered.

03 / Process

Five steps.

01
  1. 01

    Brand identity from scratch

    Name, logo, colour system and tone of voice, built from zero. Youthful and community-led, without overshadowing the artwork it exists to sell.

  2. 02

    Shopify + custom front end

    Shopify as the commerce engine, with custom Liquid, HTML, CSS and JS on top. Overrode the template wherever it got in the way: product pages, collections, artist features, the nav.

  3. 03

    Artist onboarding

    A simple submission and onboarding process for local creators. Each artist gets their own collection and full attribution on site.

  4. 04

    UGC + organic social

    Grew to 1.5K+ through customer photos and community, with no paid ads. Artist and customer relationships fed back into product drops.

  5. 05

    Ongoing iteration

    Watching behaviour and iterating: tighter product pages, a shorter checkout, artist stories surfaced across the purchase flow.
